ENKINDDLE Flameless Candle 3″ × 6″

The middle height of the range: an ivory pillar in real wax, lit by a patented 3D moving flame that flicks and sways inside a melted rim. At home on mantels and dining tables, it runs on a built-in four-hour timer and two C batteries — no smoke, soot or open flame.

  • A flame that moves A lifelike 3D flame flickers and sways the way a real candle does, set into a melted wax rim, so the candle reads as real from any seat at the table.
  • Set it once, enjoy it every day The switch on the base sets a four-hour timer. The candle comes on at the same time each evening and turns itself off after four hours, then repeats the next day.
  • Up to 600 hours on two C batteries Long battery life means fewer changes and more evenings lit. Battery life depends on use and battery quality.
  • Real wax, ivory finish Cast in real wax with a hand-finished ivory surface, the pillar has the weight and texture of a traditional candle and suits any décor.
  • Safe around children and pets No open flame, no smoke and no dripping wax — the beauty of candlelight without the risk.
  • Gift-ready packaging Each candle ships in a sturdy protective box that keeps it safe in transit and makes it ready to give.
