A flame that moves

Like the real thing, from any seat at the table

Each ENKINDDLE candle casts a soft yet vibrant glow. Its patented 3D moving flame flicks and dances inside a melted wax rim, so the light never looks flat or frozen — no matter where you are standing.

Real wax gives the pillar the weight and texture of a traditional candle, and the built-in timer means it is already lit by the time your guests arrive.

For calm, alluring & safe environments

Safe, smart & beautiful

Candlelight is beautiful when it lives in a safe, controlled space. Because ENKINDDLE candles are battery-operated, they remove the dangers of traditional ones: no accidental fires, no burn injuries, no indoor smoke.

Now everyone — from children to senior citizens — can enjoy the simple pleasure of a candle-lit room.

Spend less, save more

Traditional candles and lamps mean repeat buys and costly maintenance. An ENKINDDLE candle runs for up to 600 hours on two C batteries. With fewer batteries and replacements to buy — and lower electricity bills — hospitality and retail customers save thousands every year.
